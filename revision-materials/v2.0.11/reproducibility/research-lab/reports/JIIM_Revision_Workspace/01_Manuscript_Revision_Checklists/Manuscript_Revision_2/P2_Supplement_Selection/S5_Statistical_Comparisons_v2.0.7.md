## Candidate caption / note

Panel C reports model1 minus model2 BA in percentage points, existing paired split-then-seed percentile intervals, and conditional split-average sign-flip p-values. The primary Holm family comprises six pairs within each stratum; the all-36 adjustment is a secondary sensitivity analysis. Panel D uses a standardized negative log10(context-level top-two BA difference + 1e-6), leave-one-split-out discordance and dataset-specific intercepts. The saved fit has 10,000 resamples, zero nonconverged bootstrap fits and ridge 1e-8. Its odds ratio and data interval come from the saved identity-correct replay; no refit was performed. Original S5 Panels A/B remain supporting metrics.
